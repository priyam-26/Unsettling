# 🕯️ Unsettling

Some questions sound harmless until you realize who they are being asked to.

A person becomes a story.  
A story becomes a headline.  
And somewhere between the two, the questions begin.

What was _she_ wearing?  
Why was _she_ there?  
Why did _she_ trust?  
Why didn't _she_ leave?

We become so accustomed to asking these questions that we forget to question the question itself.

**Unsettling** is a small interactive experience built around that discomfort.

It doesn't try to explain everything.  
It doesn't try to give you an answer.

It simply asks you to slow down for a moment — to look at the questions we have learned to ask, and notice where they place the blame.

Because sometimes, the most important thing is not finding a different answer.

It is realizing that we were asking the wrong question all along.

---

## A Moment to Think

Not every story needs another question.

Sometimes, it needs understanding.  
Sometimes, it needs silence.  
And sometimes, it simply needs us to stop looking for a reason to blame the person who was hurt.

---

## Closing Thought

This project is not meant to tell you what to think.

It is meant to make you pause before asking the next question.

And perhaps, this time, ask it differently.
